⚠ Risks

  • Pace/timeline consistency is the main friction, not skillEvery project so far has been a new client or new format — Vishen's reassignment was about client strictness meeting a context-switch cost, not a quality failure
DECISION — SANKET

Hire. The quality of the work is genuinely high, and that's the harder thing to build in an editor. The friction is pace and timeline consistency, not craft.

Expect this to improve once he's working with one consistent client instead of a new one every project — he flagged this himself unprompted in his own check-in ("work consistently with the same creator/client... I wouldn't need to constantly build context, collect assets, and learn different creator styles"). Every project to date has required a fresh ramp-up, which is the most expensive version of this job. Give him that consistency and time to level up into it.
